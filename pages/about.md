# Łukasz Matlakowski Blog

My name is Łukasz and I'm here to share with you my knowledge and experience of over 20 years of working in IT.

Technology has always been more than just work for me — it is a constant journey of learning, building, and improving. I have been working with Linux for as long as I can remember, and today its the same. On desktop I use macOS, while I also work extensively with Windows technologies. I enjoy building and maintaining my own homelab, where I test new ideas, automate processes, and explore modern infrastructure solutions. Networking has always been one of my biggest passions, especially technologies such as MPLS and BGP. I like understanding how systems communicate, scale, and stay resilient in complex environments.

On this blog, I share practical knowledge, tools, and real-world experience from everyday work in IT. You will find topics related to Linux, servers, networking, cloud technologies, security, automation, and AI. I constantly follow the latest trends in technology and enjoy experimenting with modern cloud platforms and artificial intelligence solutions. I believe the best way to grow is through continuous learning, curiosity, and hands-on experience. This blog is a place where I document that journey and share it with others who are passionate about technology.
